The Championship

Compete for the Title

EBHC was created to fill a gap in Edmonton's adult ball hockey scene by building a dedicated city championship where the best teams compete for something that matters.

City Champion Titles

Championship titles awarded in every division. This is the only tournament in Edmonton built specifically to crown city champions in adult ball hockey.

Balanced Competition

Structured, tiered play so matchups stay balanced and every game means something, from the first round-robin draw to the final buzzer.

Structured Format

Full round-robin seeding followed by structured playoffs ensures every team earns their path to the final.

Tournament Details

How It Works

The EBHC format. 2027 specifics, including divisions and dates, will be confirmed with the announcement.

Venue

Servus Credit Union Place, St. Albert. Just outside Edmonton's northwest edge, played in the field houses.

Three Days

A Friday-to-Sunday weekend of championship ball hockey. Round robin first, playoffs on the final day.

Tiered Divisions

Teams compete in their skill tier. Champion titles are awarded in each division or playoff pool.

Round Robin + Playoffs

Full round-robin seeding into structured playoffs. Minimum 4-game guarantee for every team.

Championship Awards

Division champion titles and championship hardware for the winners.

Certified Officials

Professional certified referees and timekeepers for every game. No exceptions.

FAQ

Common Questions

Everything else you might want to know before 2027.

When is EBHC 2027?

Dates have not been announced yet. The 2026 edition ran August 7–9. Follow @ebhctournament or email us to hear first.

How is the tournament structured?

Teams play a full round robin, then are seeded into structured playoffs with a minimum 4-game guarantee. In 2026, the top four seeds played for the Pool B title and seeds five through eight played for the Pool C title.

How many teams can enter?

Entry is capped and spots are first-come, first-served. Once spots are filled, registration closes regardless of the deadline date. In 2026 the cap was 20 teams.

What did it cost in 2026, and what was included?

The 2026 entry fee was $1,000 per team, with a $300 non-refundable deposit at registration and the balance due before the tournament. It included certified officials and timekeepers for every game, full tournament operations, championship awards, and a minimum 4-game guarantee. 2027 pricing will be confirmed with the announcement.

How does the deposit work?

A deposit is due at registration to secure your team's spot. The remaining balance must be paid by the announced deadline, and final rosters are submitted to the organizers ahead of the weekend.

Where can I find stats, standings and replays?

Every season is on this site, filterable by year: standings and scores, Finals MVPs and stats and leaders, with links through to the official HockeyShift pages. Full game replays are on YouTube, and games are streamed live during the tournament on our Live page.

Where is the tournament held?

Servus Credit Union Place, 400 Campbell Road, St. Albert. Games run in the field houses. Full details and directions are provided to registered teams.

Who organizes the Edmonton Ball Hockey Championship?

EBHC is organized by Shinny of Champions. Team registration is managed through SOC Tournaments using the HockeyShift platform.
